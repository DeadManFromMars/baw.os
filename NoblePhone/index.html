FLIP PHONE — 3D

Drag to turn · right-drag or scroll to zoom · middle-drag to move the view · double-click to flip · click a key to press it · hold the red key 3 s to switch it on / off. Keyboard: arrows = the d-pad, Enter = OK, Esc = F1 (back), Tab = F3 (options), Backspace = CLEAR, numbers dial, and while writing just type. F1 / F2 / F3 = the soft keys under the screen (back · OK · options). Green calls, red hangs up / goes home. Book key: Contacts · envelope key: Messages · camera key: the camera (again: selfie; OK takes a picture; drag to aim, scroll to turn it sideways; CLEAR or F1: back). Writing on the keypad: tap a key for its letters (2 = a b c), ✱ capitals, # space. H hides this panel.
